## Context

CI run 39299815752 (wip/T-099 @ 28aba7a, windows job, step `cargo test -p voicen --no-fail-fast`): `src-tauri/tests/hotkey.rs::esc_is_released_after_a_max_length_stop` panicked at `src-tauri/tests/hotkey.rs:725:5` with "Esc not claimed". The other 15 tests of the `hotkey` binary passed. `esc_claimed()` (`hotkey.rs:576`) probes Esc both bare and under Ctrl+Alt, and `eventually` waits `WAIT` = 3 s (`src-tauri/tests/win32_support/mod.rs:77`). The same test passed on earlier runs, including CI 39296012262 at 3186c2b. T-099's diff does not touch `src-tauri`. The investigator decides whether this is a probe race (another process holding or freeing Esc, or the hotkey thread's claim arriving after the 3 s window) or a real gap in the claim on press, and checks `docs/failures.md` and T-009 / T-043 for an earlier case of this class.

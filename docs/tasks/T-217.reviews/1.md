## Verdict rationale

The design is sound in most respects — the image is the single place `RUSTC_WRAPPER` is set, `tw-run` mounts the shared dir only when it exists so CI and machines without the `mkdir` stay on plain rustc, stdout and exit codes are preserved (CHAR3c), and the failure branches of AC-4 have biting checks. But the task's central invariant, one READ_WRITE server at a time, does not hold once the cache reaches its cap: the lock file sits inside the directory sccache evicts from, and I reproduced two concurrent READ_WRITE servers with the built image. That is a Critical correctness finding, and the probe that should have caught it (the concurrent-build check the analysis required) was replaced by a mode-line check. P-016 CI evidence is also missing. Not a recurrence: first change in this seam, no earlier entry of the class.

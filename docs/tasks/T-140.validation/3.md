## Why FAIL

The print guards from rounds 1 and 2 hold: all of catalog 25-34 are killed. But the tests of the example check only what it must not print, and one score line. They never check that the figure it reports is right, or that the key reaches the request. SC-004 is read from that figure.

Seven mutations of `crates/voicen-core/examples/wer.rs` survive all seven `wer_example_*` tests and all `wer::tests`. Each one breaks a stated guarantee:

- The analysis invariant "wer::score(reference, hypothesis), applied to wer::normalize on both sides": 116, 117, 118, 119.
- The analysis invariant "Set-level WER is the sum of edits divided by the sum of reference words": 114, 115.
- The Acceptance line "the key from that command's environment": 122.

The cause in the fixture is that `REFERENCE` and `TRANSCRIPT` have the same word count, their words never match, and neither carries anything normalization would change. So argument order and normalization make no difference, and no test reads the total's counts or the `Authorization` header.

`wer.rs` itself is fully protected. No executable defect is known: the example does the right thing today, as the control test below shows. What is missing is the test.

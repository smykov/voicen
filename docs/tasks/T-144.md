## Context

US4 (never lose a recording), US4 #1, #5–#8, #10. Spec FR-016, FR-017, FR-018, FR-020, FR-032, SC-005, SC-006. Area core, seams `crates/voicen-core/src/call/storage.rs` / `session.rs`. Needs the store (T-125), the session (T-126) and the pass (T-138).

## Acceptance

- [ ] Failure branch: at start a `recording` folder with no owning session becomes `recovered` (offered, never started by itself), end and duration from the shorter readable track, `.opus` bytes unchanged (hash compare) (T058, T059)
- [ ] A crash at minute 20 gives at least 19 min 50 s of audio (SC-005) (T058)
- [ ] `transcribing` becomes `ready-to-transcribe` and is queued; `ready-to-transcribe` is queued oldest first (T058)
- [ ] Failure branch: a write failure mid-recording stops it, keeps the flushed audio, leaves `meta.json` at `recording`, logs `call_storage_failed` and notices `stopped{storage}` (T058)
- [ ] Free space is polled every 10 s; below 1 GB one `low_disk` warning; below 200 MB the recording stops with saving (T058)
- [ ] At 4 h the recording stops with `max_length` (T058)
- [ ] Dictation `TempAudioStore` cleanup never deletes under any call root (FR-020) (T058)
- [ ] Injected faults never panic and never change the audio (SC-006) (T058)
- [ ] Test that fails without the change: the recovery and fault tests of `call/storage.rs` / `session.rs`

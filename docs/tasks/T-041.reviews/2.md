**#1, percentile.** I checked this by reading. `assert_percentile_pair` (`energy.rs:376-408`) lays out K digital-zero frames, then N−K−20 noise frames, then 20 tone frames. The tone frames are at -51.4 dBFS (-52 dBFS tone plus -60 noise, powers summed). The premises assert N, the exact count of `-inf` frames, and that every tone frame lies in [-58, -50].
- N = 100, K = 10: the sorted index 9 is a zero frame. The floor is -70, the threshold -58, and the 20 tone frames are loud and counted. The noise frames are not loud: `a.loud == tone` asserts that.
- N = 100, K = 9: index 9 is the quietest noise frame. The floor is about -60, the threshold about -48, and nothing is loud.
- So rank 1 (the minimum), the 1st, the 5th and the 9th percentiles all give -70 for K = 9 and turn the "not speech" half red. The 11th percentile (and an index of `rank` without −1) gives about -60 for K = 10 and turns the "speech" half red.
- N = 95: `ceil(9.5) = 10` against `floor(9.5) = 9`. Rounding down gives index 8, which is a zero frame for K = 9, so the test goes red.

Every premise is deterministic (fixed seed), so none of these can pass by chance. **Closed.**

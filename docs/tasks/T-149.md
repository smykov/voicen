## Context

US6 (device changes), US6 #1–#7. Spec FR-006, FR-010, FR-012, FR-013; research R4, R21. Area core, seams `crates/voicen-core/src/call/session.rs` / `capture.rs`, with a fake `DeviceEvents` stream and the fake clock. Needs the session (T-126).

## Acceptance

- [ ] A default-output change reopens the system track on the new default; a gap of at most 2 s is filled with silence and recorded in `device_changes` and `call_device … gap_ms=` (T068, T069)
- [ ] Mic loss reopens on the default input with `mic_changed`; the call is not stopped (T068)
- [ ] Failure branch: a track not reopened within 2 s, or with no device left, records silence from then on, warns `track_lost`, logs `call_track_silent rule=lost` and retries every 2 s (T068)
- [ ] `CommunicationsCheck::differs` at start gives `communications_device_differs`; `E_NOTFOUND` counts as no difference (T068)
- [ ] More than 60 s of system silence while the mic has speech gives `cant_hear_other_party`, the mirror case `cant_hear_you`; each fires once per stretch and re-arms after speech (T068)
- [ ] Warnings stay until dismissed; a dismissed kind re-arms on its next trigger (T068)
- [ ] Test that fails without the change: the device-event tests of `call/session.rs` / `capture.rs`

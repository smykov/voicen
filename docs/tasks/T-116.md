## Acceptance

- [ ] `opus` 0.4 and `ogg` 0.9 are dependencies of `voicen-core`; `make licenses` passes and `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt` is committed with them; `pub mod call` exists (T002)
- [ ] Page 1 is BOS with only `OpusHead` (contracts/core-call.md values, granule 0); page 2 is `OpusTags` with vendor `voicen <version>` and no comments (T003)
- [ ] Granules advance by 960 per packet; a page ends every 50 packets with a flush after each page and `sync_data` every 5 s (fake clock) (T003)
- [ ] The last page carries EOS and its granule trims the tail (T003)
- [ ] 60 min of speech-like signal on both tracks takes at most 40 MB (SC-007) (T003)
- [ ] Failure branch: `UnexpectedEof` or a CRC mismatch on the last page ends recoverable data; a mismatch earlier gives a damaged file with its readable prefix; no valid `OpusHead` → unreadable; duration = last good granule − pre-skip (T003)
- [ ] Failure branch: an `io::Error` from write, flush or sync maps to `StorageError` (T003)
- [ ] `TrackDecoder::next_window(max_ms)` returns at most `max_ms` of 16 kHz audio; the encoder uses the R5 settings (T003, T004)
- [ ] Test that fails without the change: `crates/voicen-core/src/call/codec.rs` tests on real libopus

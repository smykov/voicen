## Context

`crates/voicen-core/tests/call_session.rs::indicator_changes_inside_the_lock_and_shell_requests_after_it` (a T-126 test) failed once more, in the unmutated baseline of `scripts/tw-mutants core T-149` during T-149 validation 1 (2026-11-09, main @ 486eee9b): all 20 ticks had `mic_level` 0.0. It then passed 61 runs in a row (1 failure in 62).

This is the symptom T-126 saw on 2026-11-07 (run T-126-20261107T025757Z: 1060 ticks, `mic_level` 0.0). The developer analysed it then as a test timing fault (the wait loop fed 200 ms per pass without waiting for the writer; the 64-slot channels filled and every freed slot took a zero-filled Dropped event) and rewrote the loop to at most 20 passes, one 200 ms span each, waiting for a new tick per pass (`docs/tasks/T-126.md`, "Flake fix"). In that note the fix is labelled F-018, but `docs/failures.md` F-018 is a different defect (a `settings://changed` listener subscribed after the trigger): the T-126 flake has no F-entry of its own. T-149 did not change the test; it did change the session's device thread and the mic lane (`session.rs`, `capture.rs`).

Recurrence of a known defect after its fix → this rca task (CLAUDE.md › Rules). Owner of the analysis: `problem-investigator`. Exits (`docs/process/lifecycle.md` §6): (a) code, (b) architecture review, (c) process amendment — (b) and (c) are the owner's.

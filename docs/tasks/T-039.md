## Context

Routed by decision #40 (T-004 review round 1 #6). The page side of T-037's `settings_window::open(tab, field)` and the rest of spec 004 T036 that T-004 left out: the `/settings` page focuses the control with `data-field=<field>` (switching to its tab) from `?field=` on load and on a `settings://focus {tab, field}` event; closing the window with a dirty draft asks whether to discard (needs T-037's capability for `core:window:allow-destroy` and the close request); and every field of the `SettingsView` the release-1 tabs own is rendered and round-trips through `settings_save` (spec T036). Verified with Playwright on the built UI over `e2e/support/tauriMock.ts`.

## Acceptance

- [ ] `/settings?tab=recording&field=recording.hotkey` opens the Recording tab with the hotkey control focused; a `settings://focus {tab, field}` event does the same on an open page
- [ ] Closing the window with a dirty draft asks to discard; "keep editing" keeps the window and the draft, "discard" closes it; a clean draft closes without asking
- [ ] Every field the release-1 tabs own (Engine, Recording, Output, History, General) is rendered from the view and sent unchanged in `settings_save` when untouched (one Playwright case over the core-synced fixture)
- [ ] Failure branch: a `field` that names no control on the page focuses nothing and shows no error; the page still opens on the requested tab (or Engine)
- [ ] Test that fails without the change: a new Playwright spec for focus, discard and the field round-trip

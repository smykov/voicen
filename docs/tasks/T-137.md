## Context

US2 #2, #3, #7. Spec FR-023, FR-024; research R9. Area core, seam the chunk part of `crates/voicen-core/src/call/final_pass.rs`, with scripted spans. Needs the `call` module (T-116).

## Acceptance

- [ ] Timed mode: spans with gaps under 300 ms merge; a cut falls at the end of the last ≥ 300 ms gap before 120 s; chunks are cropped to first span − 200 ms and last span + 200 ms (T044, T045)
- [ ] Failure branch: a pause-less 5 min is cut hard at 120 s with the rest carried; a window with no span yields no chunk (T044)
- [ ] Chunk length is at most 120 000 ms in timed mode and 30 000 ms in time-less mode; time-less mode makes each merged span a chunk split hard at 30 s (T044)
- [ ] A chunk is encoded as 16 kHz mono PCM16 WAV of at most 44 + 32 B/ms (3 840 044 B at 120 s, #197) (T044)
- [ ] A 1 h track gives no upload above 25 MB and chunks in call order (T044)
- [ ] Test that fails without the change: the chunk tests of `call/final_pass.rs`

## Investigation

**Frame.** Feature (spec 007 G23, T044/T045). Observed: no chunker in core. Expected: research R9 behaviour under FR-018 (one window in memory), FR-023 (<= 120 s at speech pauses, nothing sent for span-less audio), FR-024 (time-less <= 30 s phrases), R11/#187 (the chunker yields one chunk at a time; up to 3 may be in flight in G24, so up to 3 chunk AudioBuffers are alive there, not in the chunker).

**Re-verified 2026-11-04** against the current tree (T-116, T-118, T-123, T-134 DONE). Serena overview of vad/mod.rs; reads of call/mod.rs, call/codec.rs:80-86, call/transcript.rs (ChunkInput, TrackInput), call/events.rs (PassMode, Track), vad/energy.rs (`impl SpeechSpans`), test_support/spans.rs; grep (not Serena: text search over strings and doc comments) of crates/voicen-core/src for `final_pass`, `chunk`, `SpeechSpans`, `enum Track`, and of specs/007-call-mode-record for `3.84`.

**Hypotheses checked.** (1) A chunker exists elsewhere: refuted. No `final_pass` anywhere in core; `chunk` occurs only in call/transcript.rs (the consumer: `ChunkInput` takes track-relative start/end the pass already has, `merge` clamps engine times into the chunk) and local_models/download.rs (byte chunks of a download, unrelated). (2) A second path cuts audio for upload: the dictation path sends one whole recording (engine/openai.rs:109-114 `multipart_body` encodes the whole AudioBuffer, 10-minute cap); no other cutter, and transcript.rs does not cut audio. (3) Earlier fixes of this class: none in docs/failures.md F-001..F-026 or its Classes table; the other `call-final-pass` tasks (T-136, T-138, T-139, T-146, T-147) are TODO and have no failure entry. (4) WAV encoding needs new code: refuted, `audio::wav::encode` (audio/wav.rs:14) is already 16 kHz mono PCM16. (5) The span type guarantees its own shape: refuted. `SpeechSpan` has pub fields and no constructor; "sorted, non-overlapping, non-empty" is only the `SpeechSpans` doc contract, the energy impl does not merge gaps, and `ScriptedSpans` returns whatever the test scripts. So the planner normalises its input (and a test feeds it a malformed list).

**Design notes for the developer.**
- Feed: T-138 calls `next_window(TIMED_MAX_MS - carry_ms)` (`max_ms: u32`) so window + carry = 120 s; port spans are relative to that window's AudioBuffer and are offset to track ms in one place, the planner's `feed(window_start_ms, audio, spans)`.
- A span ending at a window edge and one starting at the next window's start have gap 0 and merge, so a speech run is split only by the 120 s rule, not by the decoder's window boundary.
- Chunk `start_ms`/`end_ms` are track-relative `u64`, the same units `ChunkInput` takes, so T-138 copies them across.
- The 1 h test must not allocate 1 h of audio: plan ranges only and compute WAV size as 44 + 32 x ms; one separate test encodes a real maximal chunk.
- Put `#![deny(clippy::unwrap_used, clippy::expect_used, clippy::indexing_slicing)]` on final_pass.rs as on vad, events and transcript: slice audio with `get(..)`.

**Decided (#197, 2026-10-24).** D1: depends_on T-134, reuse `vad::SpeechSpan {start_ms, end_ms: u64}`, half-open. D2: no `Track` in the planner; T-138 tags chunks. D3: the WAV bound is exactly 3 840 044 B and the spec wording becomes "44 + 32 B/ms". This spec edit has not been made yet: tasks.md T044 and research.md R9 on this branch and on `main` still read "<= 3.84 MB". It goes into the developer's commit. D4: chunks do not overlap; the limit applies after the crop, and the pad is clamped by the previous chunk's end and by the limit. D5: the time-less pad is 200 ms, clamped in the same way.

**Size.** One file, one seam, pure logic over values that now exist: not large. Evidence is complete; nothing is left open. The analysis is ready for red tests.

**Recommended exit**: n/a (feature, not rca).

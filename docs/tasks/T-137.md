## Context

US2 #2, #3, #7. Spec FR-023, FR-024; research R9. Area core, seam the chunk part of `crates/voicen-core/src/call/final_pass.rs`, with scripted spans. Needs the `call` module (T-116).

## Acceptance

- [ ] Timed mode: spans with gaps under 300 ms merge; a cut falls at the end of the last ≥ 300 ms gap before 120 s; chunks are cropped to first span − 200 ms and last span + 200 ms (T044, T045)
- [ ] Failure branch: a pause-less 5 min is cut hard at 120 s with the rest carried; a window with no span yields no chunk (T044)
- [ ] Chunk length is at most 120 000 ms in timed mode and 30 000 ms in time-less mode; time-less mode makes each merged span a chunk split hard at 30 s (T044)
- [ ] A chunk is encoded as 16 kHz mono PCM16 WAV of at most 3.84 MB (T044)
- [ ] A 1 h track gives no upload above 25 MB and chunks in call order (T044)
- [ ] Test that fails without the change: the chunk tests of `call/final_pass.rs`

## Investigation

**Frame.** Feature (spec 007 G23, T044/T045). Observed: no chunker in core. Expected: research R9 behaviour under FR-018 (one window in memory), FR-023 (<= 120 s at speech pauses, nothing sent for span-less audio), FR-024 (time-less <= 30 s phrases), R11/#187 (the chunker yields one chunk at a time; up to 3 may be in flight in G24, so up to 3 chunk AudioBuffers are alive there, not in the chunker).

**Hypotheses checked.** (1) A chunker or span type exists elsewhere: refuted (Serena overview of call/mod.rs, call/codec.rs, vad/mod.rs; grep of crates/voicen-core/src for `final_pass`, `Span`, `enum Track`). (2) A second path cuts audio for upload: the dictation path sends one whole recording (engine/openai.rs:114 `multipart_body` encodes the whole AudioBuffer) and has a 10-minute cap instead of chunking; no other cutter. (3) Earlier fixes of this class: none in docs/failures.md (F-001..F-022) or its class table. (4) WAV encoding needs new code: refuted, `audio::wav::encode` (audio/wav.rs:14) is already 16 kHz mono PCM16 and is called by the multipart path, so T-137 only bounds the samples.

**Design notes for the developer.**
- Feed: G24 calls `next_window(TIMED_MAX_MS - carry_ms)` so window + carry = 120 s; spans from the port are window-relative and are offset to track ms by the caller or the planner (one place; recommend the planner's `feed(window_start_ms, audio, spans)`).
- A span ending at a window edge and one starting at the next window's start have gap 0 and merge, so a speech run is never split by the decoder's window boundary, only by the 120 s rule.
- Spans are normalised before use (sort, merge overlaps, drop empty, clamp to the window): a real detector's output is input, not a trusted invariant.
- The 1 h test must not allocate 1 h of audio: plan ranges only and compute WAV size as 44 + 32 x ms; one separate test encodes a real maximal chunk.
- `clippy::indexing_slicing` is denied in vad and should be in final_pass too: slice audio with `get(..)`.

**Owner / orchestrator decision points (recommendation in bold).**
- **D1 span type vs T-134.** The chunker needs only a span value, not the `SpeechSpans` trait (its tests use scripted span lists; tasks.md says G23 is independent of G20). Options: (a) **T-137 `depends_on: [T-116, T-134]` and uses the value type T-134 defines in `vad` (e.g. `vad::SpeechSpan { start_ms, end_ms }`, half-open)** — one definition per shared type (P-011, the #196 (4) precedent), and `vad` must not depend on `call`; T-134's analysis should fix the shape: half-open `[start_ms, end_ms)`, ms relative to the AudioBuffer, `u64` (or `u32` widened by the caller); (b) both use `std::ops::Range<u64>` and neither defines a type: no dependency, parallel work, but no named invariant; (c) T-137 defines its own type in `call`: rejected, a second definition T-134 cannot reuse without `vad -> call`.
- **D2 `Track`.** data-model `Chunk.track: Track` comes from T-118 (IN_PROGRESS). **Recommend the planner stays track-agnostic (no T-118 dependency); G24 tags chunks with the track.** Alternative: depend on T-118.
- **D3 WAV bound.** "<= 3.84 MB" (tasks.md G23, R9) is exceeded by 44 bytes at exactly 120 000 ms (3 840 044 B). **Recommend the test asserts the exact `44 + 120 000 x 32 = 3 840 044` and the spec reads "about 3.84 MB (44 + 32 B/ms)"**; alternative: cap timed chunks at 119 998 ms to stay <= 3 840 000.
- **D4 pads at boundaries.** The spec does not say whether the +-200 ms pad may overlap the neighbour chunk or push a chunk past 120 s. **Recommend: chunks disjoint (start >= previous end), the limit measured on the cropped range, pad clamped by both** — overlap at a hard cut would send the same words twice and duplicate transcript lines.
- **D5 time-less pad.** R9 says "every merged span is a chunk, split hard at 30 s" without the pad. **Recommend the same 200 ms pad, clamped by the 30 s limit and disjointness** (consistent cropping, keeps word onsets).

**Size.** One file, one seam, pure logic: not large. Evidence is complete for the analysis; the boundary semantics (D3-D5) are the only open points.

## Context

Owner answers (decisions #59, #60): Q2 — the Silero model is downloaded in CI with a pinned SHA-256 (no binary in the repository); Q3 — real voice clips are public-domain recordings (CC0 or an explicit PD dedication), recorded in licenses/manual.json. Split from T-001 by decision #43. Decision #12 and spec 002 R-1 keep native/model code out of voicen-core, so `SileroDetector` lives in `crates/voicen-whisper` and implements core's `SpeechDetector` (T-041). The energy detector cannot reliably tell a long cough from speech; FR-12 on real audio is proven only by Silero on real clips (Windows CI) plus the owner. Two owner questions block it (see `blocked_on`).

## Acceptance

- [ ] On Windows CI, the Silero detector classifies the real speech clips as speech and the real silence / cough / keyboard clips as not speech
- [ ] The model file's integrity is checked (pinned SHA-256) and its license is listed in the notices
- [ ] Failure branch: a missing or corrupt model makes the detector unavailable, and the gate falls back to the energy detector with the `vad_fallback` warning (no panic)
- [ ] Test that fails without the change: a Windows CI test in `crates/voicen-whisper/tests/`

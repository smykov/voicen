#### What I verified
- **Locks.** The lock order is `releasing` → `state`, and nothing takes them the other way.
  - Under `state` the session calls the indicator, `SettingsService::snapshot` (an `RwLock` read; `save` takes the write lock only to swap, service.rs:440), `Paster::capture_start_window`, `AudioSource::start`, and, in `Drop`, the capture handle's drop.
  - The worker holds no session lock while it is in `run_job` or `pending()`.
  - Drop while the worker is in `run_job`: Drop sets `shutdown` and drops the only `Sender` under the lock, then joins. The worker finishes the job (job_finished, publish), and its next `recv` either sees `shutdown` (a buffered job, which is dropped) or gets `Err`. No deadlock.
  - The timer checks `shutdown` under the lock before every wait, so Drop's `notify_all` cannot be lost.
  - Spurious wake-ups: the timer loop re-reads `next_deadline` and ticks only once `now >= deadline`. `tick` clears that deadline, so it cannot busy-loop.
- **Deviation (1), `State::stopping`.** Releases are serialised, so at most one stop is in flight.
  - A publish attempted by the worker, the timer, the tray, a registration or a press during a stop only notifies the timer. `finish` then sends the net change.
  - A press during a stop shows Recording at that `finish`. With one hotkey thread this cannot happen anyway.
  - Bite: without the deferral, `hold_gives_one_paste_and_overlay_recording_processing_hidden` gets `[Recording, Hidden, Processing, Hidden]`. The added test gets `[…, Recording, Processing, Hidden, Processing, Hidden]` whenever the worker ends A within the 200 ms sleep. When the machine is slower it passes without testing the race, but it never fails falsely.
  - Accepted: it carries out T-051 Notes (1) on every thread.
- **Deviation (2), the `releasing` mutex.** Correct, and it is the reason queue order equals recording order even when releases come from different threads. Accepted.
- **Deviation (3), `Release::held` and `audio::mix_to_mono`.** `held` reads the private ticket instants; it is pinned by `duration_ms: 3000` (row 1) and `299` (row 8). `mix_to_mono` is the one mix-down rule, taken out of `from_frames` (P-010). Accepted.
- **Deviation (4), events outside the lock.** `RecordingStarted` and `RecordingEnded` are emitted before `finish` queues the job, so no job event can come before them. `CaptureFailed` is always a recording's last event, and `LogObserver` joins by `RecordingId`. Accepted: the session lock is never held across the log's file write.

## Verdict rationale

The session is correct as built.
- One FIFO worker owns the pipeline and runs `run_job` outside the lock.
- Jobs are queued inside the `finish` critical section, and releases are serialised.
- `retry_available` is read from the slot on the worker.
- Publication happens under the lock, deduplicated, with no Hidden between Recording and Processing.
- Drop is bounded, and the timer is safe against spurious wake-ups.
- I accept all five reported deviations; (5) is Open (#2).

The tests bite the Acceptance lines, and no red test was weakened. The gate and 8 repeated runs are green.

One Medium (#1) has no justification. The session calls two more ports, plus a handle drop, inside its lock than the contracts say. Those ports are the next to be implemented on Windows (T-006 capture and paste, T-012 device loss), where a deadlock would show up only on Windows CI or on the owner's PC. The fix is documentation only, in the trait docs, core-traits.md and the decision file. #3 and #4 are cheap and belong in the same round. #2 is justified and goes to a follow-up.

## Context

Follow-up 3 of decision #91. The dictation log line (FR-20) gets closed fields for post-processing: `pp=applied|skipped|off`, `pp_reason=<one of the six skip reasons>` and `pp_ms=<duration>`. This touches `events.rs`, `diag/observer.rs`, the diag_format grammar and the diag_pipeline redaction run. No prompt, transcript, reply or key ever reaches the line.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Every dictation line of a post-processed job carries `pp=` and, for a skip, `pp_reason=`, plus `pp_ms=`; a job with post-processing off carries `pp=off`.
- [ ] Failure branch: a planted prompt, transcript, reply and key never appear in the log (the diag_pipeline redaction run).
- [ ] Test that fails without the change: named in `## Tests`.

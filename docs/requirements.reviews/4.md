### Requirements roast round 4 — 2026-10-21

Scope: this is a narrowed round on v6 (commit de1b721), release 2 only. It checks two things. First, whether each round-3 finding was applied as the owner accepted it (decisions #175–#178). Second, the rows v6 changed or added: every `(v6, r3#N)` line, every `r3#N` pointer, and FR-81…FR-84. Unchanged rows were not re-roasted. Repository facts were read only where a v6 line claims them: `docs/plan.md`, `docs/tasks/T-102.md`, `src-tauri/windows/installer-hooks.nsh` and `docs/decisions/diagnostics-log.md`.

This is the second round for release 2, because round 3 was its first. Under the two-round rule this record lists the Highs that remain and does not ask for a third round.

Summary: v6 applies the round-3 answers faithfully and carefully. Of the 38 findings, 27 are closed and 11 are partly closed, and none was dropped. Two Highs remain. Both come from v6's own new lines, and each needs one owner line:

- **#1:** the calls-keeping uninstaller rule (r3#1) is in a release-1 row and belongs to no increment. Today's silent and passive uninstall also still purges everything, calls included, without asking.
- **#2:** the final pass's chunks have no size. FR-82 gives each chunk the 30 s dictation timeout, and a chunk near the 25 MB limit (about 13 min of WAV) cannot reliably finish in 30 s. With the default settings, FR-60 would then fail as a timeout.

## Pre-mortem

1. Garbage text gets pasted: hallucinations on silence, or Ctrl+Alt+V instead of a paste. Prevented by FR-12 (VAD) and FR-10 (wait for modifier release). The remaining gap is where the VAD model comes from, which is still missing (#3).
2. The app silently dies or stops listening after sleep, a headset change, or a whisper.cpp crash, and the 2-week criterion can neither pass nor be diagnosed. Prevented by FR-20, FR-26 and FR-27. Still missing: crash records for native crashes (#4) and crash retention that covers 2 weeks (#1).
3. The unsigned installer is blocked by SmartScreen or antivirus on a clean Windows 11, so success criterion 3 fails. Partly covered by OQ-02 (still open) and the RegisterHotKey working assumption in §10.

### Requirements roast round 3 — 2026-10-21

Scope: only the release-2 (call mode) parts of v5 in all ten sections. That covers FR-30…FR-80, NFR-13…NFR-22, the §9 call-mode table, §10 CA-1…CA-10 and the ID map. Release-1 lines (approved v4) were read only to find contradictions with release 2. Owner decisions #159–#174 are taken as given; a finding cites one only where the draft contradicts it or leaves it without a deliverable. This is round 1 for release 2, so the caller's two-round limit runs from here.

Summary: the transfer of the package is faithful. The ВФ/ВН → FR/NFR map is complete and correct, priorities and increments match `tz.md` §11–§12, and the package's own roast fixes are carried over. The document has no Blocker. It is NOT_READY because of 9 High findings, in three groups:

- **Contradictions with release 1:** the uninstaller deletes the calls (#1); NFR-02 conflicts with call mode (#2).
- **1a promises what its prerequisites cannot deliver:** FR-45 needs Silero (#3); FR-60 needs a different API client (#4).
- **Musts with no failure branch:** capture start (#5), exit during a recording (#6), write errors (#7), the Calls section (#9). There is also an inverted disk threshold (#8).

Most Highs already have an open question (OQ-51, OQ-54, OQ-55, OQ-57), so each needs one owner line.

## Pre-mortem

Three months after release 2, call mode has failed. The three most likely reasons:

1. **The other party is missing from real calls.** The call app plays to the communications device or a Bluetooth HFP endpoint, the loopback records the wrong device, and the owner notices only after the call. Prevented by FR-46 (60 s warning) and FR-41. Missing: the device role and the start-time warning (#16), and the muted-mic check (#17).
2. **Transcripts arrive late, wrong or not at all.** The 1a final pass hits the 25 MB limit, gets no timestamps from the chosen model, or invents text on music because Silero is not shipped. Failures pile up as `failed` calls with no clear next step. Prevented by FR-65 and FR-45. Missing: the chunked final pass (#4), Silero before 1a ends (#3), and the final-pass lifecycle (#15).
3. **Calls are lost.** An uninstall with the default "yes" deletes months of calls, a native engine fault ends a recording, or a moved storage root goes offline mid-call. Prevented by FR-47 and FR-49. Missing: keeping calls on uninstall (#1), the write-failure branch (#7), and isolating native engines during a recording (#23).

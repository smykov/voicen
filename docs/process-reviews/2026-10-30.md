## Diagnosis

**Headless stage sessions repeat the same stop.** 112 of 133 `escalate` outcomes since 2026-10-24 are at `implement`, median 0.9 minutes. Of 128 `escalate` endings in the worktree logs, 116 mention a `wip/` branch or a push within the 12 lines before them (a coarse text match, some may be incidental). Per task: T-140 20, T-120 19, T-134 18, T-117 11, T-123 9. T-192, accepted on 2026-10-25 to fix this, is still `TODO`. This is the largest waste found: 107.5 M tokens at `implement`, most of it in sessions that do nothing but stop again.

**The verify stage does not keep up.** 1 of 27 tasks since the split reached done, and 15 wait at `CODE_COMPLETE`. `ready.after: code_complete` lets new work start on top of them, so a verify failure would reopen more each day. Verification follows the `main` push for tasks that deploy, so the owner's push decision is part of this queue.

**Follow-ups grow under `review.blocking: high`.** 13 → 38. The threshold itself looks right: rounds fell, docs-drift returns fell from 64 to 0, and send-backs still carry `correctness` and `done-not-working`. The cost moved into T-060, and nothing works it. After the split the 74 findings were 54 low, 9 medium, 11 high.

**The orchestrator session is the largest single cost.** One session of 8250 minutes used 222.6 M tokens, more than all stage sessions together (187 M). The journal charges it to three tasks, so its per-task figure means nothing, but the total does.

**Known environment defects have no task.** Flakes under load are only noted in T-060 (e2e boot 5 s, `local_download` trickle, `connection_test` 2 requests). The `tw-test ui` e2e bug and the manual copying of serena's `project.yml` and `memories` into new worktrees have no task either.

**The parallel limit in the config is not the real one.** `batch.parallel` is 10, the host carries 4 (owner's limit). Today the orchestrator holds this by hand.

Fine, keep as is:

- **Shell edits of task records:** the analysis gate refused 70 on 2026-10-24, 32 on 2026-10-25 and none since the rule was accepted on 2026-10-25.
- **CI guard files:** no longer recur in the new period (recurring areas 24 → 1). The sample is small; keep watching.

## Proposals

Ordered by expected effect.

### 1. Environment: do T-192 next (task)

- **Evidence:** the 116 of 128 and 112 of 133 above; T-192 has waited five days.
- **Change:** raise T-192 to P1, so the router takes it before other `TODO` tasks. The scope stays as accepted in #208.
- **Cost:** one small task; a CI run per wip push, which P-016 requires anyway.
- **Measure:** `escalate` at `implement` per task. Expect at most one per task.

### 2. Rule: verification before new starts (project rule)

- **Evidence:** 15 at `CODE_COMPLETE`, 1 of 27 done since the split.
- **Change:** in `/teamwright:parallel auto` the orchestrator starts no new `TODO` task while more than 5 tasks wait at `CODE_COMPLETE` for verification; it verifies first. Running pipelines finish. Recorded in `PRINCIPLES.md` and `docs/decisions.md`.
- **Cost:** new work starts later; verification needs the `main` push, so the owner gets that question earlier.
- **Measure:** `CODE_COMPLETE` count and verify failures at each review.

### 3. Setting: `batch.parallel` 10 → 4

- **Evidence:** the owner's limit is 4 pipelines; the config says 10.
- **Change:** `/teamwright:settings batch.parallel 4`.
- **Cost:** none today. Raising it later is one command.
- **Measure:** `launch` never reports more than 4 running.

### 4. Environment: work the follow-ups (task)

- **Evidence:** 13 → 38 open in T-060. Keep `review.blocking: high`.
- **Change:** a `type: chore` task, P2: triage T-060's open lines, fix or close them, oldest first; each line closed as fixed, moved to a named task, or dropped with a reason.
- **Cost:** one or two tasks of reading and small fixes.
- **Measure:** open follow-ups at the next review. Below 15 means the threshold can stay.

### 5. Environment: flakes and worktree setup (task)

- **Evidence:** three flakes under load, the `tw-test ui` bug, the manual serena copy; a P-016 re-run may count only if its failure is logged as a task or F-entry.
- **Change:** a `type: chore` task, P2, for the three flakes and the `tw-test ui` bug, and a second, P3, for the worktree setup copy.
- **Cost:** two tasks.
- **Measure:** red `wip/` attempts outside the diff.

### 6. teamwright: stop re-running an unanswered stop (feedback)

- **Evidence:** the per-task counts above; `tw-blockers` reads only the main tree and does not see a task `BLOCKED` in a worktree; the orchestrator session cost above.

### Feedback draft (not sent)

**Problem.** In the parallel loop a headless stage session that stops on an owner question is started again while the question is unanswered, and stops again.

**Numbers.** In one project, 128 `escalate` endings across 14 worktrees, up to 20 per task, median session 0.9 minutes, most of a stage's 107 M tokens.

**Proposed change.**
1. The loop stops a worktree after one `escalate` whose question matches the previous one, and reports it as waiting for the owner.
2. `tw-blockers` also reads task records in registered worktrees.
3. A long orchestrator session should get its own row and a suggestion to restart at a barrier: one such session cost more than all stage sessions together.
